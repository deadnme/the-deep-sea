THE FIELD GUIDE
ABOUT THE EXPEDITION

A little context.

This is an artistic, educational journey. Creature shapes and sizes are stylized; this is not a reconstruction of a single dive. The descent expands shallower zones so every habitat has room to tell its story.

Pressure is estimated at one atmosphere per 10 metres plus surface pressure. The endpoint uses NOAA's approximate depth; measurements of Challenger Deep vary.

No sunlight reaches below about 1,000 m. Creatures in these scenes are illuminated for visibility. Mariana snailfish appear in the upper hadal zone, not at Challenger Deep.
